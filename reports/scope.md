# Scope and interpretation

**The completed work covers historical performance, business drivers and a linked quarterly scenario case.**

| Included | Important boundary |
|---|---|
| Annual three-statement extraction | 199 inputs; FY2024 balance sheet is absent from this source. |
| Revenue and margin analysis | Rounded company contributions are not exact dollar effects. |
| Quarterly scenarios and actual comparison | Retrospective reconstruction using two dated information sets. |
| Earnings and simplified cash model | Cash conversion is a proxy, not a full working-capital/debt forecast. |
| Power BI | Downloadable PBIX; no public service embed is claimed. |
| Agent execution and QA | Four executable roles in the quarterly workflow; Business Driver is a documented specialism. |

## Valuation and investment recommendation

A price target is outside the delivered model. A defensible valuation needs explicit multi-year cash flows, reinvestment, a discount rate, terminal assumptions and a valuation-date net-debt/share-count bridge. Historical earnings quality alone does not determine whether the shares are attractively priced.

## Insurance perspective

This P&G case analyses a consumer-goods company. Insurance underwriting, premium quotes and company credit approval are outside its scope. The separate Travelers case covers insurance-company financial scenarios.

## Evidence conventions

- **Reported:** A figure disclosed by the company.
- **Derived:** A calculation using disclosed inputs.
- **Assumption:** A modelling choice, not an observed fact.
- **Interpretation:** A reasoned conclusion with a stated scope.

Original report and native Excel screenshots support the annual questions. Quarterly workbook previews are explicitly labelled as renders where that is their origin. Red highlights identify evidence; chart colours also carry written labels and signs.
