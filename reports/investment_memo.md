# P&G: growth did not protect operating profit

**Financial conclusion:** Revenue growth was positive, but higher costs weakened operating profitability. Forecast review points to SG&A and non-operating income as the largest remaining earnings misses.

[Excel models](../model/README.md) · [Power BI](../dashboard/README.md) · [Detailed evidence](../analysis/README.md)

## 📊 Three findings

| Finding | Evidence | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| Sales grew; operating profit fell. | Revenue +3.3%; operating profit −3.4%; [profit bridge](../analysis/02_profit.md). | Review cost conversion alongside growth. |
| Growth was not driven by company-wide volume at the disclosed precision. | Approximately FX +2%, pricing +1%, volume unchanged; [drivers](../analysis/05_growth_drivers.md). | Test volume, pricing and currency separately. |
| Research helped one forecast modestly. | May base earnings error fell from $649.6m to $580.5m; [forecast review](../analysis/10_forecast_review.md). | Better cost information does not solve an operating-expense miss. |

## 🎯 What to investigate next

1. **Marketing and SG&A:** Test spending commitments, sales response and productivity offsets together.
2. **Gross margin:** Separate mix, product investment, restructuring and recurring cost changes.
3. **Cash conversion:** Request working-capital and payment schedules before using the cash proxy for financing decisions.

## What would change the assessment?

Sustained unit growth, better operating margin and repeatable cash conversion would support a stronger operating assessment. Higher marketing spending without corresponding growth, or persistent adverse mix, would weaken it.

This memo assesses performance and model priorities. It does not assign a share-price target or a Buy/Sell rating. The annual review and the retrospective quarterly forecast have different information dates; [scope and dates](scope.md) keep them separate.
