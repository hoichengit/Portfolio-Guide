# 🧭 Review this project in three steps

**Goal: understand the result, check the source and inspect the calculation.**

1. **Read the answer:** open [Quarterly sales target](analysis/02_quarter_sales_target.md). The first sentence states the result.
2. **Check the evidence:** expand the Evidence section. Follow the red boxes from published sales to the Excel result.
3. **Inspect the workbook:** download [PG_2_Quarter_Sales_Target.xlsx](outputs/april_guidance_sample/PG_2_Quarter_Sales_Target.xlsx) and open it in Microsoft Excel. No software installation or data connection is required beyond Excel.

## 📊 What is in each workbook?

| Workbook | Purpose | Try this |
|---|---|---|
| [Management guidance](outputs/april_guidance_sample/PG_1_Management_Guidance.xlsx) | Record the company ranges and their arithmetic midpoints. | Compare each endpoint with the highlighted announcement. |
| [Quarterly sales target](outputs/april_guidance_sample/PG_2_Quarter_Sales_Target.xlsx) | Calculate the sales needed after nine months of actuals. | Save a copy, change a growth input and check how required quarterly sales changes. |

**Amounts are USD millions.** Sales are monetary values; growth rates are percentages. The nine-month total adds three reported quarters.

## 🔎 What should reconcile?

**Nine-month actual sales + sales needed in April–June = implied full-year sales.**

Check this for the low, midpoint and high references. Review the period and metric as well as the number: FY2026 growth and April–June growth are different measures.
