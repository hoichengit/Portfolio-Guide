# P&G sales sensitivity workbook

**Purpose: measure what happens if quarterly sales growth is weaker than the company-guidance reference.**

- Open [the workbook](PG_3_Scenario_Adjustment.xlsx) and use the case selector on Assumptions.
- “Base” is the middle stress case: demand −0.25pp and pricing −0.25pp. It is not an evidence-validated central forecast.
- The broader [market review](../../analysis/03_scenario_adjustment.md) found mixed evidence and does not calibrate those steps.
- The workbook and native Excel screenshots preserve the original experiment. No new forecast or source-data worksheet is claimed.
- [Results](results.json) reproduce the original cases; use the unchanged reference alongside them when comparing with actuals.
