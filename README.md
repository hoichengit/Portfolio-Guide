# P&L Analysis

**Explain how a company's sales become profit — and whether that profit becomes cash.**

- **P&L:** revenue drivers, cost changes, margins and earnings quality.
- **Balance sheet:** liquidity, working capital, debt and asset quality.
- **Cash flow:** operating cash conversion, investment, distributions and closing-cash reconciliation.

Each project contains **nine analysis questions**, concise answers and expandable original-report / real-Excel screenshots. The key figures are highlighted in red.

Both review **FY2026 versus FY2025 actuals**. P&G closes its year on 30 June; NIKE closes on 31 May. Their periods differ, so the projects are individual reviews rather than a matched-period company ranking.
