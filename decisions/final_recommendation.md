# Final analytical conclusion

**Prioritise earnings quality and expense conversion when assessing P&G's growth.**

- **Historical finding:** Product costs and SG&A absorbed the revenue increase.
- **Forecast finding:** Research improved one base case, but SG&A and non-operating income remained the largest misses.
- **Next model action:** Build a more explicit operating-expense schedule and test cash conversion before using the model for financing decisions.

## Monitoring priorities

| Indicator | Why monitor it? | What would change the interpretation? |
|---|---|---|
| Volume and pricing | Distinguish unit demand from price-led growth. | Durable volume recovery would strengthen the growth assessment. |
| Gross margin | Track mix, costs and productivity together. | Better margin with consistent definitions would support improved conversion. |
| SG&A / sales | Detect spending that exceeds sales growth. | Better sales response or spending efficiency would reduce expense risk. |
| Cash conversion | Check whether earnings turn into cash. | Working-capital timing can explain apparent strength or weakness. |

This is a financial-performance conclusion, not a priced investment recommendation. A Buy/Sell view requires a completed valuation and investment-date assumptions.
