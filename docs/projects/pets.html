Budget vs Actual · INDEPENDENT PROJECT

Pets & More

Budget & Forecast Analysis

Budget vs actual variances, full-year forecasting, recovery scenarios and cash funding requirements.

Download ExcelView dashboard

What I worked with

  • Pets & More, LLC: a small pet retail and services business described in the original portfolio dataset. This is an independent analysis of public project data; the source does not establish a verified company identity.
  • 55 actual income and expense records, January–May 2022.
  • 108 monthly budget records across nine categories, January–December 2022.
  • Dates, categories, descriptions and amounts in USD.

What I did

  • Matched actual and budget periods and categories.
  • Explained monthly and year-to-date variances.
  • Built forecast, recovery and cash-planning exercises.

Two identical $300 insurance entries and potential timing/completeness issues are flagged; no unsupported correction is posted.
